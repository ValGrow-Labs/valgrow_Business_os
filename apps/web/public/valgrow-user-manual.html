1. Getting Started Essential

Welcome to ValGrow Business OS — a unified multi-branch Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Point of Sale (POS) system designed for retail, wholesale, and multi-location businesses.

Initial Access & Navigation Steps

  1. Open the Application: Launch the app in any modern browser via ValGrow Login.
  2. Login: Enter your authorized email address and password.
  3. Main Dashboard: Upon logging in, you arrive at the central Overview dashboard showing business key performance metrics.
  4. Left Sidebar Navigation: Access modules grouped into Operations (POS, Products, Inventory, Purchasing, Customers, Reports) and Management (Organization, Branches).
  5. Global Command Search: Press Ctrl + K or click Global Search to quickly navigate to any module.
Role & Permissions Note: Access permissions are assigned to your user account (e.g., Owner, Store Manager, Cashier). If a specific feature or button is disabled, contact your administrator to verify your permission settings.

Verification Checklist

  • Login page loads cleanly without errors
  • Authorized credentials log in successfully
  • Overview dashboard renders greeting and key cards
  • Sidebar navigation items expand and route smoothly

4. Branches & Warehouses Structure

ValGrow Business OS is engineered natively for multi-branch organizations. Operational branches represent physical store locations or hubs, while warehouses manage actual inventory storage facilities.

Branch Management

Navigate to Organization → Branches to create and manage operational branch locations.

  • Create Branch: Click New branch and specify Branch Name, Branch Code, City, Address, Phone, Email, and Status (ACTIVE or DRAFT).
  • Branch Assignment: Every POS terminal session and inventory movement is linked to a designated branch.

Warehouse Configuration & Selection

View storage depots under Inventory → Warehouses.

Configuration Note: Warehouse availability and assignment depend on the current system configuration. If a separate warehouse creation option is not visible in your interface, contact your system administrator. Users select their assigned warehouse during POS register opening and Purchasing / Goods Receipts.

5. Inventory & Stock Stock Control

The Inventory module provides real-time visibility into stock quantities across warehouses, low-stock warnings, and manual stock adjustment mechanisms.

Stock Terminology

  • On-Hand Quantity: Total physical units currently held in the warehouse.
  • Reorder Level: Threshold below which a product is flagged as Low Stock.
  • Stock Status: IN_STOCK, LOW_STOCK, or OUT_OF_STOCK.

How to Perform a Manual Stock Adjustment

  1. Navigate to Inventory.
  2. Locate the target product line.
  3. Click Adjust Stock (or the adjustment icon).
  4. Select the adjustment type (e.g. Add Stock / Damage / Correction).
  5. Enter the quantity adjustment and mandatory reason note.
  6. Click Save Adjustment and verify the updated On-Hand stock figure.

CSV Export Action

Click Export on the Inventory table to generate a downloadable CSV of all current inventory levels and valuations.

Verification Checklist

  • Inventory list displays correct On-Hand stock levels
  • Low-stock items display warning status badge
  • Stock adjustment modal updates On-Hand quantity
  • Inventory CSV export downloads successfully

6. Purchasing & Purchase Orders Procurement

Purchasing governs supplier procurement, Purchase Order (PO) creation, and approval workflows prior to receiving goods into stock.

1. Select Supplier ➔ 2. Create PO (Draft) ➔ 3. Approve PO ➔ 4. Goods Receipt ➔ 5. Inventory In

How to Create & Approve a Purchase Order

  1. Go to Purchasing → Purchase Orders.
  2. Click New Purchase Order.
  3. Select the Supplier and target Destination Warehouse.
  4. Add line items, entering Ordered Quantity and Unit Cost Price.
  5. Click Create Order (saved as DRAFT or SUBMITTED).
  6. An authorized manager clicks Approve PO to transition status to APPROVED.
Example Purchase Order
PO Number: PO-2026-0042
Supplier: Apex Wholesalers Ltd
Warehouse: Central Distribution Hub
Items: 50x Test Rice @ ₹40.00 = Total ₹2,000.00
Status: APPROVED

Verification Checklist

  • PO creation modal populates suppliers and items
  • Saving PO records line items and total calculation
  • Approving PO changes status to APPROVED

7. Goods Receipts (GRN) Stock Receipt

Goods Receipts (Goods Received Note / GRN) confirm the actual physical receipt of items ordered via an approved Purchase Order.

Receiving & Posting Stock Workflow

  1. Navigate to Purchasing → Goods Receipts.
  2. Click New Goods Receipt.
  3. Select the eligible APPROVED Purchase Order from the dropdown.
  4. Verify or adjust the Received Quantity for each item line.
  5. Save the draft GRN.
  6. Click Post Goods Receipt to finalize receipt.
Expected Result: Upon posting the Goods Receipt, system stock levels for the received items in the designated warehouse are automatically increased by the received quantity.

Verification Checklist

  • Approved PO appears in Goods Receipt selection
  • Received quantities can be verified or adjusted
  • Posting GRN increases physical inventory On-Hand quantity

8. POS Register & Barcode Scanner Core Sales

The Point of Sale (POS) register is optimized for fast, high-volume retail checkouts, barcode scanning, cart management, and immediate sales completion.

POS Operational Flow

Open Session ➔ Scan / Select Product ➔ Add Customer ➔ Checkout ➔ Payment ➔ Print Receipt

1. Opening a Register Session

If no register session is active, the POS screen prompts you to open one:

  • Select Branch and Warehouse.
  • Enter Terminal ID (e.g., REG-01) and Opening Cash Float (e.g., ₹1,000.00).
  • Click Open Register Session.

2. USB Barcode Scanner Workflow

ValGrow Business OS includes full global USB barcode scanner support:

  1. Plug any standard USB barcode scanner into your PC/POS terminal.
  2. Open the POS register screen (ensure session is open).
  3. Scan the product barcode directly — no manual field focus required.
  4. The scanned item is instantly added to the cart (or quantity incremented by 1 if already in cart).
  5. If an invalid barcode is scanned, an error toast notification sounds/alerts.
Barcode Scan Action Expected System Result
Valid barcode scanned Product added to active cart with quantity 1
Same barcode scanned twice Cart item quantity increments to 2
Unrecognized barcode Warning toast: "Product barcode not found"

3. Cart Actions & Checkout

  • Quantity Adjust: Click + or - buttons or edit quantity directly.
  • Hold / Resume Cart: Pause an active cart to serve another customer and resume later.
  • Customer Selection: Search and select a registered customer for loyalty tracking.
  • Checkout Modal: Click Checkout (F9), select Payment Method (CASH, CARD, UPI, STORE_CREDIT), enter tendered amount, view change due, and click Complete Sale.
  • Receipt: View, download, or print the formatted invoice receipt.

Verification Checklist

  • Register session opens with float amount
  • USB barcode scanner adds products to cart on scan
  • Scanning existing item increments quantity
  • Hold and Resume cart functions correctly
  • Checkout processes payment and calculates change
  • Completed sale reduces inventory stock automatically
  • Printable sales receipt generates cleanly

15. Troubleshooting Support

Common operational questions and resolutions:

Problem: Product not appearing in POS search/scan

Check: (1) Product Status is ACTIVE. (2) Barcode match is exact. (3) Product has available inventory in the active POS warehouse.

Problem: USB Barcode scanner not adding items to cart

Check: (1) USB scanner cable is securely plugged in. (2) POS register session is active. (3) Scanner is set to standard USB HID keyboard mode.

Problem: Cannot post Goods Receipt

Check: (1) Corresponding Purchase Order has status APPROVED. (2) Target warehouse is active.
